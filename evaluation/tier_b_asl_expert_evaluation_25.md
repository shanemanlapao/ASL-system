## 📖 Evaluation Guidelines & Rating Criteria

Please evaluate each generated ASL gloss translation based on the **TAUS / LDC Machine Translation Quality Framework** using the two criteria below:

### 1. Adequacy (Semantic Meaning Preservation)
*How much of the original English meaning is successfully expressed in the ASL gloss?*
* **5 = All Meaning Preserved:** 100% of the meaning is conveyed; no information is lost.
* **4 = Most Meaning Preserved:** The essential message is intact; only minor nuances are omitted.
* **3 = Much Meaning Preserved:** The core intent is understandable, but some details are missing or distorted.
* **2 = Little Meaning Preserved:** Major information loss; difficult to understand the original intent.
* **1 = None / Inaccurate:** Completely wrong meaning or incomprehensible.

### 2. Fluency (ASL Grammar & Syntactic Flow)
*How well does the gloss adhere to authentic American Sign Language (ASL) grammar?*
*(e.g., Topic-Comment structure, Time-First order, absence of English linking verbs/articles, proper Wh-word placement)*
* **5 = Flawless ASL Grammar:** Natural sign order; strictly follows ASL grammatical conventions.
* **4 = Good ASL Grammar:** Easily understandable as sign language; minor word-order awkwardness.
* **3 = Understandable:** Message is conveyed, but heavily mimics spoken English word order.
* **2 = Difficult to Sign:** Awkward phrasing that feels unnatural or confusing to sign.
* **1 = Incoherent / Unintelligible:** Random or incorrect sign sequences.
